The 99 Beautiful Names

Search, filter, and tap any Name to explore its meaning, reflection, and a practical action for today.

Names, Arabic script and transliteration are cross-checked against standard Asma-ul-Husna references. The enumeration of 99 Names is recorded in Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī (3507) and Sunan Ibn Mājah (3861); the promise tied to knowing them is in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī (2736) and Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim (2677). Some scholars note the specific ordered list is a later addition to the narration rather than the Prophet ﷺ's exact wording — the Names themselves are firmly rooted in the Qur'an and Sunnah regardless. Qur'an references are shown only where the association is well established; where none is shown, the Name is affirmed through the broader Qur'anic and prophetic tradition rather than one specific verse. This app is an educational companion, not a substitute for study with a qualified teacher.
